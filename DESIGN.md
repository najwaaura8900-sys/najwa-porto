## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, ambitious, and refined identity for an emerging Visual Communication Design (DKV) creative from SMK Negeri 10 Surabaya. Balancing energetic youthful optimism with disciplined editorial maturity, the aesthetic pairs a multi-stop sunset gradient with crisp structured layouts.

The design movement mixes **Editorial Minimalism** and **Bento Grid Architecture**:
- **Tone:** Creative, warm, bold, and meticulously structured.
- **Audience:** Creative directors, design agencies, prospective clients, and academic evaluators looking for fresh yet professional design talent.
- **Motifs:** Four-point geometric sparkles used sparingly as micro-accents, delicate hairline boundaries, fluid pill badges, and commanding high-contrast dark accents to anchor luminous sunset hues.

## Colors

The palette is driven by a radiant, 5-stop linear progression: Deep Rose (`#DB2265`) through Coral (`#FF5969`), Persimmon (`#FF8840`), Apricot (`#FFBB63`), to Soft Champagne (`#FFDB99`). 

Application rules:
- **Primary Brand Gradient:** Applied as background fills for hero banners, focal callouts, key active indicators, and text-fill masks on display typography.
- **Dark Surface (`#171820`):** Acts as the primary anchor for high-contrast presentation cards, key interactive buttons, and primary typography, grounding the warm palette.
- **Canvas (`#FFFDFC`):** A warm off-white canvas prevents visual fatigue and avoids sterile clinical whites while maintaining contrast.
- **Borders (`#E9E3E5`):** Retains a subtle rosy-gray undertone to harmonize cards with surrounding warm tones.

## Typography

The type system blends the contemporary, geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and impact statements with the neutral, hyper-legible utility of **Inter** for descriptions, bento metadata, and interface interactions.

- Headlines leverage tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and solid leading (`1.05` to `1.2`) to produce an authoritative editorial texture.
- Body copy relies on generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) in `text-muted` (`#686A73`) or deep slate (`#171820`), ensuring maximum comfort in multi-paragraph project case studies.
- Uppercase tracking (`0.03em`) is reserved exclusively for small pill chips, project categories, and date stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric **12-column Bento Grid** designed to package diverse media formats (branding mockups, motion clips, project context, skills, and accolades) into self-contained modular containers.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width 1280px, 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 48px (`3rem`) page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6 columns with 20px gutters, adapting multi-span bento modules into 2-column or 3-column rows.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single column or 2-column micro-grid with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and canvas padding.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8px baseline rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px, `space-2xl` = 48px). Bento tiles should maintain consistent internal padding of `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile tonal boundaries and soft ambient coloration rather than generic drop shadows:

- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Standard cards use a crisp 1px solid border (`#E9E3E5`) against the `#FFFDFC` canvas with a pure `#FFFFFF` background.
- **Warm Ambient Glow:** For hovering interactive cards, apply an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with brand warmth: `0 16px 32px -12px rgba(219, 34, 101, 0.12)`.
- **Inverted Bento Anchors:** High-priority cards (such as "About", "Featured Hero Work", or "Contact") utilize a dark surface (`#171820`) with light typography and subtle gradient inner borders or glows (`0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Floating Island Navigation:** The header navigation bar floats in a translucent pill container: `rgba(255, 253, 252, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a 1px border of `rgba(233, 227, 229, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances soft, tactile corners with elongated pill capsules:

- **Bento Cards & Containers:** Standardize on 16px (`rounded-lg`) up to 24px radius, creating smooth module corners that sit comfortably within the grid.
- **Interactive Controls & Badges:** Primary buttons, navigation menus, and category chips utilize full pill roundedness (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Input Fields & Form Elements:** Standardize on 12px (`rounded-md`) to bridge between structural cards and soft interactive controls.
- **Sparkle Motifs:** Graphic starburst accents utilize four-point concave geometry, placed adjacent to key headings or floating as subtle background watermarks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid dark pill (`#171820`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`), or a linear gradient fill (`#DB2265` to `#FF8840`) with white text for primary conversions. Height 48px, horizontal padding 24px (`space-lg`), font `label-md`. On hover, scale up slightly (`transform: scale(1.02)`) with a warm ambient glow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pill-shaped border of 1px solid `#E9E3E5` on `#FFFFFF` with `#171820` text. Hover changes border to `#DB2265` and tints background with 5% rose opacity.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Chips:** Ultra-compact pill badges (`rounded-full`), height 28px, padding 4px 12px. Neutral variation uses `#F4F0F1` with `#171820` text. Active/Specialty variation uses a subtle background gradient (`#FFDB99` to `#FFBB63` at 20% opacity) with `#DB2265` text.

### Bento Cards
- **Portfolio Tile:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid `#E9E3E5`, 20px corner radius. Includes image display area with 12px internal radius, overlaid tag pills, project title, and category label.
- **Dark Anchor Bento:** Deep tone (`#171820`), white text, featuring subtle gradient mesh accents in the corner and four-point sparkle iconography in `#FFBB63`.

### Navigation Pill
- Centered or right-aligned floating dock. Contains icon or text link items with 8px radius or full pill hover highlights. Active navigation item is marked by a solid `#171820` background pill with `#FFFFFF` text or a micro gradient dot.

### Form Inputs
- 12px radius, 1px border (`#E9E3E5`), background `#FFFFFF`. Height 44px, padding 0 16px. On focus: border color switches to `#DB2265` with a 3px ring of `rgba(219, 34, 101, 0.15)`.

### Four-Point Sparkle Iconography
- Used as vector graphic touches (16px to 24px inline, or 48px+ decorative background watermark) rendered in `#FF8840` or masked with the signature multi-stop gradient.
